# AcademicOS Phase 1 Pilot Agreement

Pilot terms and conditions, mutual non-disclosure, and data sharing. Version phase1-v1, 4 October 2026.

This agreement is between the school named when it is accepted (the "School") and Vidyuth Labs, which operates AcademicOS ("AcademicOS", "we", "us"). The School's principal, or another person the School has authorised, accepts it for the School in the AcademicOS web console. AcademicOS records who accepted, their designation, the date and time, and a fingerprint of this exact text. An agreement accepted electronically in this way is valid under the Information Technology Act, 2000.

## Part A. Pilot terms and conditions

1. What Phase 1 is. Phase 1 is the first pilot of AcademicOS at the School, run through the web console by the principal: setting up the school (classes, sections, subjects and staff), the timetable and calendar, lesson plans, question papers and answer keys from the AcademicOS question bank, marks, and reports. Other parts of AcademicOS, such as the teacher and student apps and messages to parents, are switched on during Phase 1 only when both parties agree, by email.

2. How long it runs. Phase 1 starts when this agreement is accepted and runs until 31 March 2027, the end of the 2026-27 academic year. Either party may end it earlier by giving the other 15 days' notice by email.

3. No fees. Phase 1 is free of charge. Nothing in Phase 1 turns into a paid plan automatically; any paid plan after it needs a separate written agreement.

4. Accounts. The School decides who gets an account and which role. Each person keeps their password and sign-in codes private. The School tells us straight away if it believes an account has been misused, and we help it lock the account.

5. Fair use. The School uses AcademicOS only for its own teaching and administration. It does not copy, resell or bulk-download the question bank, try to get at the software's source code, test its security without our written permission, or upload material it has no right to use.

6. A pilot, not a finished product. AcademicOS is being actively developed. Features may change, be added or be removed during Phase 1, and the service can have errors, including in generated papers, answer keys, marks, timetables and reports. The School's teachers review every paper and answer key before it is used, and remain responsible for marks and grades. We aim to keep AcademicOS available during school hours and tell the principal in advance of planned downtime, but Phase 1 has no guaranteed availability.

7. Feedback. The School agrees to tell us what works and what does not, including in a short check-in about every two weeks. We may use that feedback to improve AcademicOS without any obligation to the School. Feedback does not include the School's personal data, which Part C covers.

8. Ownership. AcademicOS, its software, question bank, designs and documentation remain ours. The School's own material (its records, the papers its teachers write, its logo and its data) remains the School's, and the School allows us to store and process it only to provide AcademicOS to the School. Question bank material based on NCERT and CBSE publications is provided for the School's internal educational use only.

9. Liability. Phase 1 is provided free and as it is. Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit. Our total liability for Phase 1 is limited to INR 10,000. Nothing in this agreement limits liability that the law does not allow to be limited.

10. When Phase 1 ends. For 30 days after Phase 1 ends, the School can download its papers, answer keys, marks and reports from the app, and can ask us for a copy of the rest of its data. After that we delete the School's personal data within 30 days, and from backups as they expire, unless the School asks us in writing to keep its account for a next phase or the law requires us to keep something. We confirm the deletion by email.

## Part B. Confidentiality (mutual non-disclosure)

11. What is confidential. "Confidential Information" means anything one party shares with the other during Phase 1 that is not public. For the School this includes its student, parent and staff data, school records, internal papers and assessments. For AcademicOS this includes unreleased features, the software, the question bank, product plans and pricing, and the terms of this pilot.

12. What each party does with it. Each party uses the other's Confidential Information only for Phase 1; protects it at least as carefully as its own, and never less than reasonably carefully; shares it only with its own people and advisers who need it for Phase 1 and are bound to keep it confidential; and does not give it to anyone else without the other party's written permission.

13. What is not covered. These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, that the receiving party already had or developed independently, or that it received lawfully from someone else free to share it. A party may disclose Confidential Information when the law or a court requires it, after telling the other party where the law allows.

14. How long it lasts. These obligations last during Phase 1 and for three years after it ends. For personal data they last for as long as the data is held.

15. Returning it. When Phase 1 ends, or earlier on request, each party returns or deletes the other's Confidential Information, except what the law requires it to keep.

16. Publicity. We do not name the School as a user of AcademicOS, or use its name or logo, in public without the School's permission by email. The School does not publish screenshots or descriptions of unreleased AcademicOS features without our permission by email.

## Part C. Data sharing and protection

17. Roles. For the personal data the School puts into AcademicOS, the School is the Data Fiduciary and AcademicOS is its Data Processor under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We process that data only on the School's instructions, which are given by using AcademicOS and by email.

18. The School agrees to share its data. For Phase 1, the School shares with AcademicOS the data AcademicOS needs to run for it: staff names, email addresses and roles; students' names, classes, sections and roll numbers; parents' and guardians' names and email addresses where parent accounts are used; timetables, calendars and lesson plans; question papers, homework, answers and marks; and scanned answer sheets if the School uses scanning.

19. Consent for children. Every student is a child under the law. The School is responsible for having a lawful basis for the data it enters, including the verifiable consent of a parent or guardian where the law requires it. The School records that consent in AcademicOS, and AcademicOS does not process a student's scanned work, AI marking or practice without it.

20. What we use it for. We use the School's data only to provide AcademicOS to the School and to fix and improve it for the School. We may also use statistics that identify no person, such as how many papers were made or which screens were slow, to improve AcademicOS in general. We do not sell the School's data, show advertising, track or monitor children's behaviour, build profiles of students for any purpose other than the School's own teaching, or train AI models on data that identifies a student.

21. Where it is kept, and how. The School's data is stored on Google Cloud in Mumbai, India, encrypted in transit and at rest. Only the people who operate AcademicOS can reach it, and only to provide or fix the service. AcademicOS keeps a log of grading and administrative actions.

22. Who else receives it. Google Cloud and Firebase host AcademicOS and its database. Sarvam AI receives scanned answer sheets and answer text, without a student identifier added, only when the School uses scan reading or AI marking. Composio and Gmail send the email AcademicOS sends, such as sign-in codes and notices, and so receive the recipient's address and the message. Google receives a sign-in when someone chooses Sign in with Google. We tell the School by email before adding any other service that would receive student data.

23. If something goes wrong. If we become aware of a breach affecting the School's personal data, we tell the principal without undue delay, and within 24 hours, with what we know and what we are doing about it, and we help the School meet its own duties to report it.

24. People's requests. We help the School answer requests from students, parents and staff to see, correct or delete their data. The principal can see and erase the data AcademicOS holds about a student or a parent from the console.

25. A fuller agreement. The parties may sign AcademicOS's full Data Processing Agreement. If they do, it takes precedence over this Part C on data protection.

## Part D. General

26. Changes. This is the whole agreement for Phase 1. If we need to change it, or for a later phase, we show the new version in AcademicOS and ask the principal to accept it; until then this version applies.

27. Law and disputes. Indian law governs this agreement. The parties first try to settle any dispute by talking in good faith for 30 days, and after that it goes to the courts in India that have jurisdiction.

28. Notices. Notices are given by email: to the School at the email address of the principal's AcademicOS account, and to AcademicOS by replying to the person at AcademicOS who sent the School its invitation.

By selecting I agree, the person accepting confirms that they have read this agreement and are authorised to accept it for the School.
